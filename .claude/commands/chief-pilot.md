---
description: The chief pilot review: hours against limits, duty and rest, currency and ratings
---

Run `crew-hours`, `currency`, `duties` and `crew-check` with `--json`. Write the review to `drafts/chief-pilot-YYYY-MM-DD.md`: anyone over or within 10 percent of a limit, rest below the minimum, medicals and checks due in 45 days, pilots not current for passengers, trips that need a different pilot. Cite the rule for each from docs/compliance.md.

Ambiguous names list the candidates and stop: ask which one. Never send anything; drafts stay in `drafts/`.
